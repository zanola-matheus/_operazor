# Operazor

**Financial and operational management for barbershops and barbershop chains.**

Operazor replaces spreadsheets with one place to track revenue, barber performance and profitability. Owners record weekly production per barber and their finances, and Operazor turns it into a dashboard, a P&L (DRE) and a cash flow report.

## What it does

### Operations
- **Dashboard:** real-time revenue, average ticket, appointment count and days worked, with year-over-year comparison and a barber ranking
- **Weekly production tracking:** per-barber revenue, appointments, services, product sales and days worked, split between walk-in clients and club subscribers
- **Targets and pace:** monthly revenue targets per unit, or weekly targets per barber, with status tracking and month-end projection
- **Occupancy:** per-barber chair occupancy from daily capacity data, classified by performance level
- **Customer retention:** new-client rate and return rate by time window (30, 60 and 60+ days)
- **Subscription clubs:** revenue by plan tier, subscriber movement, monthly churn and estimated LTV

### Finance
- **P&L (DRE):** generated automatically from financial entries, with monthly columns, percentage analysis and comparison against the trailing average
- **Cash flow:** opening and closing balances per bank account, reconciled against real balances
- **Budget vs. actual:** monthly targets per category, compared with realised figures
- **Entries:** instalments (up to 120), recurring entries, receipt attachments, and cost splitting across units

### Multi-unit and teams
- **Networks:** standalone shops, or HQ with branches and consolidated reporting
- **Roles:** Master, Manager, Operator and Viewer, each with scoped access to units and reports
- **Data in and out:** Excel import with an error-correction panel, and Excel export
